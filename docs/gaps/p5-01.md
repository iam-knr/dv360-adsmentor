## Deviations
- New SIM codes used by the console: SIM-016 (series over 26 windows), SIM-017 (invalid date or time), SIM-018 (only
  upcoming windows can be changed), SIM-019 (repeat needs days and an end date), SIM-020 (class not found, also for a
  class the person does not teach), SIM-021 (choose a student). SIM-004B is the 100 character text of SIM-004.
- Deleting an upcoming window sets it to `CANCELLED` (the row stays), as the module 18 conflict C3 describes.
- Editing a series changes label, note and time of day of this and later upcoming windows, never the dates.
- Roster "Remove" needs the typed word REMOVE in the form; the service still takes `confirm: 'DELETE'` internally.
