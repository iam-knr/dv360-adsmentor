# P4-06 gap list (YouTube line items, ad groups and ads)

What module 09 still lacks after the P4-06 PR, and what other modules need to do for it. Size: S under half a day,
M about a day, L two days or more. Task ids are module 09 tasks (`docs/specs/modules/09-youtube.md` section 7).

## A. Not done in module 09

## C. Deviations from the module 09 spec

- The ad group bid field of a Demand Gen group is hidden and not required (its bid follows the line item strategy, 4.6).
- `Ad.youtubeVideoId` is nullable and `Ad.imageAssetIds` is new (migration `0007_youtube_demand_gen`), because Demand Gen image and carousel ads have no video.
- Δ-09-7 (`AdSequenceStep`) already exists in the schema; nothing writes it (A4).
- The line item exposure cap for YouTube uses `freqMode UNLIMITED` ("No limit"); other types still store `INHERIT`.
